Calm vs Energy Cards

A 60-second reset for real life. Help kids notice what they need right now: Calm or healthy Energy.

Best moments to use it

  • After-school crash
  • Before bedtime transitions
  • Before homework / focus time
  • When “I’m bored” turns into chaos

How it works

  1. Choose: Calm or Energy.
  2. Do one tiny action together.
  3. Repeat tomorrow—kids learn through repetition.
