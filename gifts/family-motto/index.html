Family Motto Cards

Small phrase. Big impact. Family mottos are short sentences you repeat until they become your family’s default. They help kids remember what to do when emotions spike, motivation drops, or everyone starts arguing.

How to use (1 minute)

  1. Pick 1 motto for the week.
  2. Repeat it once a day (breakfast is perfect).
  3. When a real moment hits, use the same phrase again (calm voice, same words).

Best for

  • Morning routines and transitions
  • Encouragement during hard moments
  • Building cooperation and a “team” mindset

Pair it with: World 01 (big feelings + coping skills) or World 02 (habits + routines).

FAQ

What are Family Motto Cards?

They are short, repeatable phrases your family uses daily. Repetition helps kids remember them in real moments.

When should we use them?

Use them at breakfast, before school, after school, or right before a tough transition like bedtime or homework.

Do they work for reluctant readers?

Yes. They are short and simple, designed to be remembered and repeated - not studied.
