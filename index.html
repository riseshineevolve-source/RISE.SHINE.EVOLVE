The Happy-Makers!

24 GENTLE STEPS TO CHRISTMAS

A Mindful Family Journey to Togetherness,
Reflection & the Magic of Christmas

Create magical December memories with 24 Gentle Steps to Christmas - a mindful, heartwarming advent experience for the whole family.
Each day invites you to spend around 10 minutes together through simple, meaningful practices that awaken gratitude, laughter and love.
From shared reflections to mindful breathing and joyful games, these short rituals help families reconnect and discover the true spirit of the holidays.

Includes:
24 daily family activities:
Mindful Moment, Fun Spark, Family Connect
Weekly inspirational quotes and daily mantras
Printable Advent Cards section for DIY calendars
Beautiful tone for all ages — children and adults alike
No preparations, no equipment required! Just open minds and hearts needed.
A journey of stillness, warmth, and togetherness — one gentle step at a time.
