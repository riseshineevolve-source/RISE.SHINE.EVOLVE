FAQ

What is Level Up Your Brain?

A story-based life-skills series for busy families. Each level combines a short story, a simple brain explanation (Neuro-Coaching Console), and a practical cheat code you can try right away.

Which book should we start with?

Start with World 01 for core coping skills and big feelings. Choose World 02 if your biggest pain points are routines, habits, screen time, and the digital world.

Is this only about emotions?

No. The series covers emotions, but also habits, routines, friendship, self-care, teamwork, focus, and digital-life skills.
