The Confident, Mindful & Happy Me Adventure

A 31-day guided journey for kids to build self-esteem, practice mindfulness, and learn simple tools for big feelings—designed for real, busy family life.

What’s inside

  • Daily prompts that build confidence and self-respect
  • Mindful check-ins that help kids notice and name feelings
  • Growth mindset language (“I can learn this”) and resilience practice
  • Simple routines that support calmer days

How families use it

  1. Do one short page a day (10 minutes of connection).
  2. Pick one tiny action for the day.
  3. Repeat for 31 days and keep the best tools.

FAQ

Is this a storybook?
It’s a guided 31-day adventure with daily prompts designed to build confidence, mindfulness, and growth mindset.

How long does it take each day?
Just a few minutes. Most families do one page a day and pick one tiny action to practice.

Who is it for?
Kids who need support with confidence, mindset, and big feelings—and parents who want a simple daily routine that actually sticks.
