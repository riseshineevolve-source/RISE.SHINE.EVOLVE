Privacy Policy

Rise Shine Evolve Last updated: 18 September 2026

This policy explains how the Rise Shine Evolve website handles personal data when you browse the site, choose analytics, subscribe to updates, or contact us.

Google Analytics is off by default on this website. The Google Analytics tag is loaded only after you actively choose Allow analytics. Advertising storage, Google Signals, remarketing and ad personalization are not enabled as part of this setup.

1. Who is responsible for this website?

Rise Shine Evolve operates this website. For privacy questions or requests, contact rise.shine.evolve@gmail.com.

2. What information may be processed?

Website analytics, only after consent. If you allow analytics, Google Analytics 4 may process information about how the website is used, such as:

  • pages viewed and basic interaction events;
  • browser, device and operating-system information;
  • referral/source information and approximate geographic information;
  • online identifiers and analytics cookies or similar identifiers used by Google Analytics.

We use this information to understand which pages and resources are useful and to improve the website. We do not use this GA4 setup for advertising personalization, remarketing or Google Signals.

Newsletter or community sign-up. If you subscribe, the information you provide may include your first name, email address and communication preferences.

Messages you send us. If you contact us directly, we process the information contained in your message so we can reply.

3. Legal basis

For Google Analytics on this website, the basis is your consent. Analytics remains off unless you choose to allow it. For optional newsletter or similar sign-up communications, we also rely on the consent you provide through the relevant form.

You may withdraw analytics consent at any time. The website provides a persistent Privacy choices control for that purpose.

4. Google Analytics 4

We use Google Analytics 4 with measurement ID G-WW6C0S6031 on the production Rise Shine Evolve website. The implementation uses privacy-first Basic Consent Mode v2:

  • analytics_storage is denied until you allow analytics;
  • ad_storage, ad_user_data and ad_personalization remain denied;
  • Google Signals and ad-personalization signals remain disabled;
  • the Google tag is not loaded on preview/local environments;
  • Global Privacy Control is respected by keeping analytics off.

The GA4 event-data retention setting for this property is 14 months.

5. Consent choice storage and cookies

The site stores your analytics choice locally in your browser so it can remember whether you allowed or rejected analytics. This choice is separate from Google Analytics itself.

If you reject analytics, the Google Analytics tag is not loaded. If you later withdraw a previous analytics grant, the site updates consent to denied for future measurement and makes a best-effort attempt to remove first-party Google Analytics cookies set on this domain.

6. Providers and international processing

Google provides the analytics service. When analytics is enabled, Google may process analytics data in countries outside your own jurisdiction. Any such processing is subject to the provider's applicable data-protection and transfer safeguards.

Other service providers used for optional features such as newsletter delivery may process the information required to provide those services. We do not sell personal data to advertisers.

7. How long data is kept

  • GA4 event data: the property is configured for 14-month event-data retention.
  • Analytics consent choice: kept in your browser until you change it or clear browser storage.
  • Newsletter data: kept while needed to provide the subscription, unless you unsubscribe or a longer period is required by law.
  • Direct messages: kept only as long as reasonably necessary to respond and maintain relevant records.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, and in some circumstances to receive a portable copy of data. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent.

You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority. In Poland, the supervisory authority is the Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych (UODO).

9. Children and family content

Rise Shine Evolve publishes educational content for families, but this website does not require a child to enable analytics. Analytics is off until consent is given. Where a feature asks for personal information, children should use it only with appropriate parent or guardian involvement.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website, analytics setup or service providers change. The current version and update date will be published on this page.
