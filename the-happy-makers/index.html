The Happy Makers

Funny stories • Real-life skills • Built for busy families

The Happy Makers are the chaotic, lovable family inside Level Up Your Brain. Their missions feel like hilarious stories, but each one quietly teaches kids and parents practical tools: coping skills, emotional regulation, routines, habits, screen time balance, and better daily choices.

FAQ

Who are The Happy Makers?

The Happy Makers are the relatable family inside the Level Up Your Brain books. Their story missions help kids and parents practice real-life skills.

Is this for kids or parents?

Both. Kids follow the stories, and parents use the short Neuro-Coaching Console and Secret Family Code to guide real-life moments.

What makes the books easy to use?

Short levels, simple brain explanations, and practical cheat codes designed for busy family routines.
