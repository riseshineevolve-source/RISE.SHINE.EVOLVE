## Amazon description draft

You did not open an ordinary puzzle book. You opened a Detective Academy case file — and the Academy has been waiting for its missing detective.

Across 30 connected missions, follow evidence, test alibis, crack codes, read visual clues, and solve deduction maps. Each mission gives you the facts you need; the job is to notice what they mean. Keep your case files nearby, because a solved case can still matter later.

Start with training, grow from Rookie to Field Agent and Master Detective, and follow the Happy Makers squad through an Academy mystery built for readers who want more than disconnected activities.

Stuck? The 3-level Hint Vault gives a nudge without taking away the thrill of the solve. Clear reasoning solutions help young detectives see how the evidence fits together.

For ages 8–12, this is a premium, screen-free mystery adventure designed for pencil marks, big ideas, and the moment a child says: “Wait — I know what happened.”

Join Happy Makers Detective Academy. Your case file is ready.
