# Happy Makers — V3 character voice bible

Status: production writing contract. The six established characters and their jobs are preserved; the relationship beats below develop those roles for V3. They do not add relatives, biographies, abilities or puzzle knowledge absent from the evidence.

Authority: the September 2026 V3 owner brief, `PROJECT_BRIEF.md`, `HMDA_MASTER_PRODUCT_LOCK.md`, the squad descriptions in `content/book_en.yml`, and current phase-source dialogue. Older editorial locks yield where V3 explicitly requests a rewrite. No separate Happy Me / World 01 / World 02 text was used as a source for this document.

## The ensemble

The Happy Makers investigate because they enjoy making useful things, helping people and figuring out how the Academy works. Their shared history appears through habits and comfortable disagreement, not invented backstory lectures. Mimi organizes a team already full of ideas; Luli checks those ideas; Dilo and Alio test different kinds of possibilities; Nini keeps the people in view; Bibi connects present evidence to incomplete historical context. The reader supplies the decisive inference.

Use `HAPPY MAKERS CHAT` for character exchange. Put critical testimony in `WITNESS BOARD` or `EVIDENCE LOCKER`, even when a character first notices it in dialogue. A joke is not an extra rule. Unsupported theories are visibly challenged before the reader must solve anything. Nobody lies to the reader for a punchline.

## Luli — Lead Clue Analyst / Logic Guardian

| Dimension | Direction |
|---|---|
| Traits | Precise, curious, steady, dryly funny; capable of changing her mind. |
| Speech | Short claim, exact question, small correction. Uses ordinary words rather than courtroom lectures. |
| Notices first | A contradiction, a missing comparison, or a fact being promoted into a conclusion. |
| Good at | Elimination, naming the next useful test, admitting what evidence cannot establish. |
| Bad at | Leaving a messy hypothesis alone long enough for someone to explain it. |
| Annoyed by | Confidence offered as proof; answers declared before all constraints are checked. |
| Laughs at | An extravagant theory meeting a very small physical obstacle. |
| Teases | Punctures the claim, never the person. Gives credit when a wild idea survives a test. |
| Usual friction | Dilo's impressive certainty and Alio's preferred shortcuts. |
| Natural support | Nini's distinction between mistaken and dishonest; the reader's careful annotations. |
| Joke rhythm | Large theory / tiny factual reply. Vary who gets the last word. |
| Never | “Obviously, ” “Anyone could solve this, ” insults about intelligence, a permanent sneer, or a complete solution before the verdict. |

Voice sample: Dilo: “The scanner has developed a grudge.” Luli: “Against which barcode?” The question opens a test; it does not ridicule Dilo.

Growth: early Luli guards the evidence; by Cases 26–30 she trusts the reader to apply the method without narrating every move. Let her say “I missed that” once when Nini notices absence. She is not infallible.

## Dilo — Code & Tech Agent

| Dimension | Direction |
|---|---|
| Traits | Inventive, enthusiastic, competitive, resilient, extravagantly confident. |
| Speech | Punchy declarations; device or game comparisons; a hurried qualification after correction. |
| Notices first | A beep, a number sequence, an interface, an unexplained device state. |
| Good at | Codes, calibration, testing a mechanism, trying again after a failed hypothesis. |
| Bad at | Separating how exciting a theory is from how likely it is. |
| Annoyed by | Tangled cables, labels that do not match controls, being told his “upgrade” needs a pencil. |
| Laughs at | His own elaborate title being reduced to a practical task. |
| Teases | Gives harmless tasks inflated technical names; accepts the same treatment back. |
| Usual friction | Luli's requests for evidence and Mimi's simpler workable plans. |
| Natural support | Nini when a witness needs patience; the reader facing a difficult code. |
| Joke rhythm | Grand claim / correction / more modest grand claim. Use this shape sparingly. |
| Never | Hacking as magic, unexplained surveillance, invented statistics presented as fact, superiority over a child, or ignoring a real safety warning for laughs. |

Voice sample: “I have recalibrated my confidence.” Mimi: “And the clock?” Dilo: “Also the clock.”

Growth: the early “legendary security” boast becomes a late willingness to check. On the final map he offers the reader a clean evidence list instead of another prediction. His Book 2 curiosity opens a harmless file drawer, not a dangerous locked compartment.

## Alio — Field Agent

| Dimension | Direction |
|---|---|
| Traits | Bold, physical, curious, dramatic, surprisingly observant when he pauses. |
| Speech | Action verbs, enthusiastic proposals, specific practical discoveries. |
| Notices first | Routes, entrances, distances, where a body or object could actually fit. |
| Good at | Testing routes, reading space, finding a useful question hidden inside an improbable idea. |
| Bad at | Checking whether the shortest line is a legal route before announcing it. |
| Annoyed by | Waiting while everyone discusses a place they could inspect. |
| Laughs at | His own elaborate expedition meeting a wardrobe, hedge or ordinary locked door. |
| Teases | Overstates the expedition, then joins the work when corrected. |
| Usual friction | Mimi's sequencing and Luli's need for physical evidence. |
| Natural support | Bibi with old plans; the reader doing a difficult spatial task. |
| Joke rhythm | Dramatic setup / prosaic result. Sometimes Alio delivers the factual correction himself. |
| Never | Dangerous stunts presented as instructions, mocking caution, a tunnel in every scene, or being reduced to the team's fool. |

Voice sample: “I checked the exciting shortcut. It is still a wall. Taking the useful one.”

Growth: after the route case he checks access before celebrating. The old-map payoff rewards looking carefully at a sealed space; it does not prove an unsupported underground tunnel. His helmet can return once in the finale as a familiar prop.

## Nini — People & Courage Specialist

| Dimension | Direction |
|---|---|
| Traits | Warm, perceptive, patient, quietly brave, capable of a sharp small joke. |
| Speech | Concrete observations and open questions; reassuring without praise inflation. |
| Notices first | Someone's hesitation, a distinction the others skipped, what is absent. |
| Good at | Fair interviews, reframing a stuck moment, noticing a small discrepancy without shouting. |
| Bad at | Giving up an appealing harmless explanation immediately; asking for room to speak in a noisy debate. |
| Annoyed by | Calling a mistaken witness a liar; treating speed as proof of skill. |
| Laughs at | A pompous label attached to an ordinary object or a parrot's ordinary needs. |
| Teases | Offers a gentle literal translation of someone else's grand claim. |
| Usual friction | Dilo jumping from inconsistency to accusation. |
| Natural support | Luli's careful distinctions; Mimi when the evidence bag becomes chaos. |
| Joke rhythm | A quiet observation that changes the whole exchange. |
| Never | Baby talk, endless “You are amazing, ” false reassurance, diagnosing a witness, or treating every difficulty as an emotional crisis. |

Voice sample: “We can ask again. A clock can be wrong without anyone being a villain.”

Growth: Nini's careful questions become essential in Case 26. She notices the empty-room possibility; the reader must test all fourteen maps and derive the message. Nini does not give the initials away.

## Mimi — Case Captain

| Dimension | Direction |
|---|---|
| Traits | Practical, decisive, inventive, affectionate, personally untidy in small ways. |
| Speech | A clear next action; a short list when chaos needs structure; occasional mock-official phrasing. |
| Notices first | What is missing from the process, which evidence needs protecting, who needs a job. |
| Good at | Priorities, evidence handling, getting everyone moving together. |
| Bad at | Applying her perfect filing system to her own pencil, phone or crowded bag. |
| Annoyed by | A technical solution longer than the practical one; evidence mixed with lunch. |
| Laughs at | Her own rule being gently applied to her. |
| Teases | Gives a wildly ambitious plan a perfectly sensible budget or checklist. |
| Usual friction | Alio's immediate expeditions and Dilo's unnecessary upgrades. |
| Natural support | Nini's witness care; Luli when a case needs a firm stop-and-check moment. |
| Joke rhythm | Official declaration / personal exception discovered / cheerful correction. |
| Never | A scolding teacher voice, threat of punishment, “because I said so, ” or competence erased by repeated lost-item jokes. |

Voice sample: “The evidence is organized. The pencil is conducting an independent investigation.”

Growth: she invites the reader into a manageable first task, then increasingly hands over the case wall. At the end she recognizes what the reader actually did, rather than awarding generic cleverness.

## Grandma Bibi — Adventure Keeper

| Dimension | Direction |
|---|---|
| Traits | Adventurous, observant, playful, dignified, candid about uncertain memory. |
| Speech | A vivid specific recollection, a pause where certainty stops, dry replies to affectionate teasing. |
| Notices first | An old mark, construction detail, familiar handwriting, a changed name. |
| Good at | Historical context, finding retained objects, recognizing what should be checked. |
| Bad at | Remembering exact dates or the whole old system from one fragment. |
| Annoyed by | Guessing an answer on her behalf; treating her as either all-knowing or helpless. |
| Laughs at | Her old squad's overambitious inventions and her own enormous photograph hat. |
| Teases | Turns a remark back gently; leaves the other character room to answer. |
| Usual friction | Dilo demanding a complete answer from an incomplete memory. |
| Natural support | Alio's interest in places; Nini's willingness to leave uncertainty open. |
| Joke rhythm | Warm recollection / unexpected practical detail. Avoid an oracle's cryptic pronouncements. |
| Never | Withholding a known essential clue “to test you, ” ageist jokes, magical memory on demand, or solving the final lock for the reader. |

Voice sample: “I remember building it. Whether we remembered an off switch is a separate question.”

Growth: fragments become a history the team can verify against documents. The final routing log supplies the causal proof; Bibi explains its context. The next-case file can surprise her without undoing Book 1's closure.

## Reader relationship and comedy controls

- Before the invitation, address the reader as the person holding the book. Do not say the reader already has an ID or belongs to the squad.
- At the single join step, use the reader-written detective name. Early cases call the reader “recruit” or “field detective.”
- Middle cases can say “our detective” when the team relies on a recorded observation. Late cases must require the reader's earlier work. Membership is earned by actions.
- Praise the method: “You kept the positions. We can check them.” Avoid “genius, ” rankings against other children, and guaranteed claims about feelings.
- Usually use two or three short chat lines. Quiet beats and one-line reactions are allowed. Do not force a joke into the Rule Zero discovery or final identity decision.
- Do not repeat the same speaker order more than twice consecutively. Give Nini and Bibi moments that change the next action; counts alone do not prove balance.
- The witness ledger, objective, rules and answer never depend on understanding sarcasm. Put a mistaken theory in chat and an explicit correction before it could affect the puzzle.
- Final review test: hide speaker names and read representative exchanges. Their concerns and rhythm should identify them; accents, catchphrases and typography must not do all the work.
