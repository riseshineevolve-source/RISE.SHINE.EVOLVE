## Premise and promise

The Happy Makers run a lively Academy investigation desk. Their small, real cases concern misplaced belongings, muddled records, mixed-up labels, surprising messages and people whose memories need checking. An old recruitment packet reveals a vacant detective place. A message addressed to **THE PERSON HOLDING THIS BOOK** invites the reader to help temporarily. Thirty cases later, the reader has enough evidence to discover why the vacancy existed, what Room Zero was and why their own completed book is the missing part of the investigation.

The central question changes as evidence accumulates: **Who is missing? → Why this mark? → What does zero mean? → What did our solved maps leave empty? → Who belongs at the empty hook?** Each change must arise from something the reader has actually seen and recorded.

The Happy Makers supply access, partial observations, competing ideas and companionship. The reader places the witnesses, checks the alternatives and makes the verdict. Bibi supplies historical context, never an unearned answer. The present-day squad contains six Happy Makers; the reader becomes its newest member. Five historical badges on six old hooks refer to Bibi's original training squad, not a missing member of the current six-character cast.

## Locked answers and boundaries

- Thirty ordered cases; fifteen spatial modules; the fourteen earlier spatial cases feed the meta puzzle. Case 29 does not add a fifteenth letter.
- Empty ROOM initials, in Cases 02, 04, 06, 07, 10, 12, 13, 15, 17, 19, 20, 22, 23, 25, spell **CHECK THE OLD MAP**. ZONES do not count.
- The final spatial answer is **Vega at D3**. Source identity Vera and the locked geometry remain unchanged.
- Rule Zero remains **ZERO ASSUMPTIONS. NOTICE FIRST. THEORIZE SECOND.** Its full wording is withheld from onboarding and earned in Case 28.
- The final stages use ASSUMPTIONS, D3, BALL–STAR–BOLT–HEART, and the reader's chosen detective name. The old-map location and reader-as-next-detective payoff remain intact.
- Book 1 closes its central mystery. **CASE 001 // STILL OPEN** begins a separate series investigation afterward.
- No external watcher orchestrates the incidents. No magic mark, invented culprit, secret identity replacement or new victim is added to rescue a weak explanation.

## Causal account: what actually happened

1. Bibi and four childhood squadmates used the old training room to begin cases with zero assumptions. Their initiation route ended at an empty NEXT DETECTIVE hook. Renovation sealed the room, leaving the route unfinished; a retained plan and archived case records survived.
2. The modern Academy still uses an intake station derived from the old record system. Its equipment produces labels, evidence tags, contact-sheet backs, routing slips and archived file inserts. The old recruitment packet and engraved badge are historical objects, not freshly conjured evidence.
3. Registering the solved opening training file satisfies an old start condition and wakes the dormant initiation queue. A dated intake log records that event. The code does not need consciousness, knowledge of the child's identity or access to events outside the Academy's records.
4. Some files are live calls; others reopen real Academy incidents. The queue selects existing record packets whose verified witness maps carry its stored empty-room pattern. It copies their recorded evidence and routes those packets in the intended sequence. It does not cause the incidents, move people, alter testimony or retrospectively change an answer.
5. The same intake stamp and inherited templates physically put the plain 0 on Academy-controlled paperwork. A new paper evidence sleeve around an old object can carry a new mark without the object changing. Map labels and recorded placements are fixed before the reader sees that case.
6. The evidence queue has selected fourteen fitting map records; the reader still must solve them and notice their unused ROOMs. The system's selection explains the letter sequence without requiring unrelated people to have arranged their lives around a decades-old acrostic.
7. The finale exposes a dated routing/selection log, the physical stamp/template source and the queued training instructions. That evidence supports the causal explanation. Bibi can then recognize the work of her old squad. Nobody has been secretly staging all thirty events.
8. The badge invitation left the reader's identity blank on purpose. The missing place is a role waiting to be filled, not an adult secretly missing from the current team. The name the child wrote at the join step belongs at NEXT DETECTIVE.

**Adopted Case 05 linkage (applied to canonical source by the production lead):** the final terminal uses Dilo's Case 05 field-locker sequence. The modern intake session records the active field-locker sequence in its dated routing log in Case 05, then the final access panel asks for that recorded sequence. Do not imply an untouched ancient lock predicted Dilo's modern choice. The FIELD LOCKER LINKED record establishes the connection without revealing its later use.

## Case transitions and pacing

The case matrix provides all thirty causal handoffs. Strong local links include trophy → comparison photos; robot → bag contents; parrot/backstage records → witness timing; old photo → Academy history; ticket → clock correction; torn note → empty-space hypothesis; fourteen maps → acrostic; old map → Rule Zero → access → reader name.

A transition may also be a new real request for help while the team carries the larger question forward. Do not pretend every parcel, theatre rehearsal and wildlife camera caused the next incident. The intake queue explains selection and ordering; character reactions explain why the team keeps investigating.

Use a stable case backbone with varied page composition:

- `CASE FILE` and `WHAT HAPPENED` establish the situation.
- `YOUR OBJECTIVE` is the dominant action instruction.
- `HAPPY MAKERS CHAT` has a reason to appear and does not carry hidden rules.
- `INVESTIGATION RULES` appears before the puzzle.
- `WITNESS BOARD` holds people/statement evidence; `EVIDENCE LOCKER` holds object or document evidence.
- `YOUR VERDICT` is a white writing field before answer checking.
- `SIGNAL LOG` varies by case and can share a page; it need not be a giant standalone zero page.

Short evidence cases may use two pages; larger cases three; selected record/reveal beats four. Five consecutive cases must not share the same page rhythm, chat shape and signal treatment. Use a real signal log, rank beat or case index to repair facing parity; never insert unexplained filler.

## The reader's book becomes evidence

From the first spatial case, instruct: **WHEN YOU SOLVE IT: Write each witness number in the final square. Keep your completed map.** Do not mention empty rooms or initials yet. Put the instruction before or beside the final writing area, never only in backmatter.

At Case 21, the note earns the idea of absence. At Case 26, give a fourteen-row lookup ledger with case number and generated page reference. Use the current build's page index, not hardcoded V2 pages. Count only named spaces marked ROOM. The reader extracts the letters; the puzzle page must not contain the assembled phrase.

The Hint Vault groups all Level 1 hints first, then Level 2, then Level 3. Explain “Try one level, return to your case, then decide whether you need more.” Solutions remain a distinct section. A turn-the-book answer treatment is optional only after a readable print-safe prototype; it does not replace deliberate navigation.

## Reveal and earned recognition

Before giving the explanation, show the reader enough to inspect: the inactive-era training plan, the Case 01 reactivation timestamp, case-packet routing rows, stamp/template output, and the NEXT DETECTIVE hook. Avoid an explanatory paragraph that simply says “the system did it.”

Suggested closing voice, to be fitted to the final evidence art:

> The routing log began with your first solved file. The old Academy had not made the trophy disappear or muddled anyone's labels. Those cases were real. It had gathered their records, printed its old mark and put the right files in your path.
>
> “We built a test that waited for someone to finish it, ” Bibi said.
>
> Mimi looked at the maps you had kept. “And you did.”

Certificate core: **FOR SOLVING THE MYSTERY OF ROOM ZERO BY FOLLOWING THE EVIDENCE ALL THE WAY TO THE TRUTH.** Recognition comes from the Happy Makers. Keep one writable detective-name line and a clear date field; remove the “certificate committee” voice.

## Book 2: show the new case

Book 1 resolution and recognition occur first. Then Dilo opens the ordinary file drawer established in the closing scene. The file inside is stamped **CASE 001 // STILL OPEN** and bears a clearly different triangular archive symbol, never the plain 0. Inside is a team photograph with one place cut away; its caption reads **RETURN BEFORE THE FIRST MEETING**. Bibi recognizes the file cover, not the missing answer. Luli reads the caption twice. Alio quietly puts his helmet back on. Mimi leaves space beside the file for the reader's pencil.

Final invitation: **“Detective, ” Mimi said, “are you still with us?”**

The production lead adopted this concrete Book 2 seed for V3. It does not define Book 2's culprit, time-travel rules or solution. Keep the impossible-seeming caption unexplained. Do not reopen the solved cause of Book 1 or claim its thirty incidents were staged.
