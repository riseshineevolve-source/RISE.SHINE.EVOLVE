## The immediate premise

Five established field detectives work at the Happy Makers Detective Academy: **Mimi, Luli, Dilo, Alio and Nini**. **Grandma Bibi is the archive mentor**, outside the field-detective slots. The active certification rack has six hooks. The first five hold those five current detectives' badges; hook 06 is vacant. The reader is invited to train for **Detective Six**.

The reader is not an existing missing person, a replacement for Bibi or a secret seventh field detective. The letter makes the offered identity clear. What remains to be earned is official certification, and what remains mysterious is the meaning and cause of Room Zero's trail. The finale does not pretend the reader's identity was unknown after the invitation.

People bring the team ordinary mysteries with worthwhile stakes: muddled labels, belongings, records, routes, access and remembered events. The Academy supplies real new calls and reopened incident records. A solved spatial snapshot establishes the requested contact or access relation. It does not, by itself, prove guilt or a particular handover.
