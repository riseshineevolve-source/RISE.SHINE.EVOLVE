# HMDA Book 1 V3 — Owner upgrade plan

Status: **IMPLEMENTED FOR OWNER REVIEW** on 22 September 2026.

V3 turns the thirty-case collection into one causal Academy investigation while preserving the verified case answers. The production target is a 145-page, US Letter, grayscale interior that can be reviewed digitally and then proved physically before KDP submission.

## Editorial contract

1. Preserve every accepted puzzle answer and deduction that remains sound.
2. Repair ambiguity at the smallest evidence-bearing point. Cases 01 and 05 receive explicit constraint repairs; Cases 03, 08, 09, 10, 11, 16, 21, 22, 23, 24, 27, 28, 29 and 30 receive clarification or evidence-scope repairs.
3. Make the recurring mark a causal investigation. Each Signal Log must add evidence, reject a theory, unlock an archive step or move the team toward Room Zero.
4. Let the Happy Makers act as a team with distinct voices. The reader joins their work gradually and earns access through recorded deductions.
5. Vary case rhythm. V3 uses compact two-page cases, standard three-page cases, four-page spatial/artifact sequences and deliberate parity pauses.
6. Keep the interior grayscale, pencil-friendly and code-driven. Canva remains a finishing layer only.

## Pagination contract

Every spatial board starts on an even printed page and its map follows on the odd facing page. The pairs are 14/15, 20/21, 26/27, 30/31, 38/39, 44/45, 48/49, 54/55, 62/63, 68/69, 72/73, 80/81, 84/85, 90/91 and 102/103. `FIELD PAUSE // PREP THE BOARD` pages are intentional production controls, not filler.
